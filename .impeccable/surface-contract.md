# GymTracker — Field Notes with terracotta

Mode: Operate. Android phone-first. Code-led native implementation with the explicitly user-approved image as fidelity reference. The user approved the visual direction and authorised implementation.

THESIS: A warm training logbook makes any exercise immediately accessible, then gives kg/reps entry protected focus. Equipment availability determines execution order. Numbers identify sets only; no exercise sequence or Next exercise action.

OWN-WORLD: Cream #fefae0 ground, olive #606c38 primary controls, forest #283618 ink, terracotta #bc6c25 prominent headings and chart/progress accents, tan #dda15e supporting tones. Bundled Lora serif headings, clear native sans-serif controls. Simple 10–12 dp outlines, 20 dp gutters, minimum 48 dp targets. Dark mode translates the same identity into forest ground, cream ink and lighter olive/copper for contrast.

STORY: Choose a plan, open whichever exercise is available, record completed sets, return to the unnumbered checklist, revisit any exercise, review actual sets, finish, compare recorded progress. Resume retains saved entries without choosing a compulsory next station.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Checklist shows the plan name in terracotta serif, editable date, completion count, a thin actual-set progress bar, and tappable exercise rows with untouched/partial/completed/skipped states. Review workout is olive. Logger pairs editable kg/reps fields and returns with Back to workout. Stats keeps range and exercise selectors, totals, clear metric choices, large best value, copper chart and exact records in one scroll flow.

FORM: Field Notes is the user-pinned direction and outranks the concept roll (seed 6249f073). Selected over Forest and Clay Studio, then explicitly approved with restrained terracotta accents. Mock values are illustrative; the app calculates actual totals. Keep validation, editing, removal, local autosave and native Back/keyboard controls even where the image omits them.

FINISH: Native light and dark/enlarged-text visual checks, compact/wide widget checks, measured contrast and any-order persistence.
