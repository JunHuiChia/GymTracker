# Grounded interface candidates

Mode: Operate. Android Material 3 structure, controls and navigation remain mandatory.

Mechanism: reusable plans become independent dated records with one row per set.
Scene: one-handed phone use between sets, followed by reviewing progress at home.
Category rut: generic fitness dashboard with motivational cards; its predictable opposite is a decorative black-and-neon gym poster.

1. Training notebook: ordered exercises and ruled set rows; familiar to lifters and immediately editable.
2. Meet score sheet: tabular load/reps/results; turns entries into an auditable record.
3. Circuit station board: clear next exercise and station order; supports progressing through a plan.
4. Equipment console: large explicit values and focused controls; suits quick input under effort.
5. Coach's weekly timetable: session dates and calendar cadence; strengthens history navigation.
6. Sports results bulletin: compact performance summaries and trend evidence; strongest for reviewing stats.
7. Library index: searchable movement catalogue with consistent classifications; strongest for plan authoring.
